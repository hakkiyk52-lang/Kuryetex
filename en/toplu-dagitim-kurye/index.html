Bulk Delivery Courier Istanbul

One list, hundreds of addresses

Chocolate boxes for 200 employees at New Year, flowers for customers on Mother's Day, gift packs for dealers at Eid, invitations for an opening… Kuryetex handles bulk deliveries to up to 500–600 addresses within Istanbul. We have couriers in all 39 districts; for large lists several couriers deliver at the same time and the list is completed the same day.

How bulk delivery works

  1. Send your list: recipient name, phone, address and any note, on WhatsApp or as an Excel file.
  2. Tell us where to collect: your office, a shop, a bakery or a florist. We can also buy the products for you.
  3. We plan the route: addresses are grouped by area and shared between couriers.
  4. You see every delivery: a delivery photo is taken at each door and sent to you; the courier also shares their live location on WhatsApp.

We buy the gift and arrange the surprise

No time to go to the shop? We go for you: tell us what you want and your budget, we send photos of the options on WhatsApp, buy your choice, have it gift-wrapped and deliver it with your note card. We don't sell products; we buy them on your behalf. You pay for the product and the courier fee after delivery. Surprise deliveries are handed over at the door without calling ahead, and midnight surprises are possible: Kuryetex works 24/7.

Special days calendar

  • New Year (31 December): corporate gift boxes, chocolates and hampers for employees and customers.
  • Valentine's Day (14 February): flowers, chocolates and gifts, delivered right on time.
  • International Women's Day (8 March): bulk flower delivery to offices.
  • Ramadan and Eid al-Adha: sweets, chocolate and gift boxes for family and partners.
  • Mother's Day and Father's Day: flowers, cakes and surprise gifts.
  • Teachers' Day (24 November): bulk flowers and gifts for schools.
  • Birthdays, anniversaries, engagements and weddings: cakes, flowers, gifts and invitation delivery.
  • Corporate events: invitations, catalogues and promotional items for openings and launches.

Important: bulk delivery organisations must be arranged with us in advance. Single-address gifts and surprise deliveries are also available the same day, 24/7.

For companies

Kuryetex is tax-registered and holds a P1* courier licence from the Turkish Ministry of Transport. We issue e-invoices and offer weekly or monthly current accounts, and we are responsible for every parcel from pickup to delivery.

FAQ

Can I track the courier live?

Yes, every order includes live tracking. From pickup to delivery the courier shares their live location with your number on WhatsApp, so you see where your delivery is on the map. The courier takes a photo at delivery.

How can I send New Year gifts to 100 employees in Istanbul?

Send the names, phone numbers and addresses as a list on WhatsApp or in Excel. We collect the gifts from your office or a shop, split the addresses by area and deliver them with several couriers on the same day. You receive a photo of every delivery and an e-invoice.

Can you buy the chocolates or gifts for us?

Yes. Tell us what you want and your budget; we send photos of the options, buy your choice, have it gift-wrapped and deliver it. We don't sell products, we buy them on your behalf. You pay for the product and the courier fee after delivery.

How many addresses can you deliver to?

We handle bulk deliveries to up to 500–600 addresses within Istanbul. With couriers in all 39 districts, several couriers deliver large lists at the same time. For organisations of this size, please contact us in advance so we can plan couriers and routes for your date.

Do you do surprise deliveries on Mother's Day or Valentine's Day?

Yes. We collect the flowers, cake or gift from the shop and hand it over at the door without calling ahead, then send you a photo. Kuryetex works 24/7, so midnight surprises are possible too.

Can you deliver flowers in bulk for Women's Day or New Year?

Yes. We collect the flowers from your florist or one we choose for you and hand them to offices, employees or customers. Flowers travel without being crushed, and you receive a photo of every delivery.

Do you deliver wedding or opening invitations?

Yes. We collect your invitations and hand-deliver them to every address on your guest list, with a delivery photo for each. Long lists are shared between several couriers on the same day.
