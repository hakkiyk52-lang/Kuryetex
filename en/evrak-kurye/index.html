Document Courier Istanbul

What is a document courier?

A document courier takes contracts, invoices, petitions, files and other official papers from your address and hands them directly to the recipient on the same day. Unlike cargo, the document does not wait in transfer centres.

It is the practical choice for offices, law firms, accountants and individuals who need a paper signed, delivered or collected quickly and safely.

What can be sent?

  • Contracts and signed documents
  • Invoices, dispatch notes and accounting papers
  • Petitions and official files
  • Keys, cards and small important items
  • Not carried: illegal items, weapons, live animals and perishable food

How an order works

  1. Choose route and delivery time in the calculator.
  2. Press the WhatsApp button and send the addresses and recipient phone.
  3. The courier collects the document and delivers it by hand.
  4. You are informed when delivery is complete.
