        FAQ

        What people ask

        Do you operate 24/7?

        Yes, we operate 24/7. You can write on WhatsApp or call at any hour, including nights and weekends. At night, courier availability and timing are confirmed on WhatsApp.

        How do I call a courier?

        Choose the pickup and delivery districts in the calculator, enter the parcel size and weight, and press the WhatsApp button. The message arrives ready; just type the addresses and send. You can also call 0551 531 52 34.

        How is the courier fee determined?

        The fee depends on the distance between pickup and delivery points, parcel size and weight. The calculator shows an estimate; the final price is confirmed when you order.

        Which districts do you serve?

        We serve the districts on Istanbul's European and Anatolian sides and Gebze. We also ship between cities such as Bursa, Tekirdağ, Sakarya, İzmir and Muğla (Bodrum); you can see an estimate in the calculator and confirm the exact price on WhatsApp.

        How much weight can a motorcycle courier carry?

        There is no extra fee up to 5 kg. For heavier shipments, 15 TL is added for each kg above 5 kg. For very large or heavy shipments, check on WhatsApp before ordering.

        Is there a separate service for pharmacies?

        Yes. In the pharmacy courier service, medicine and health products are carried in sealed packaging and handed to the recipient. Agreed pricing is possible for pharmacies with regular shipments.

        What is the difference between a document courier and cargo?

        Cargo passes through transfer hubs and can take several days. A document courier picks up your document and aims to hand it directly to the recipient on the same day.

        How long does delivery take?

        It depends on distance, traffic, weather and demand, so we share a realistic plan for each order when you place it.

        What if the recipient cannot be reached?

        The courier calls you and we decide together: retry, hand over to another person, or return. Parcels are never left with unauthorised people without your permission.

        Do you offer regular courier service for companies?

        Yes. Regular shipping agreements are available for pharmacies, offices, shops and e-commerce businesses. Just write to us on WhatsApp.

        What can't you carry?

        Illegal items, explosive and flammable materials, weapons, live animals and perishable food are not carried. Tell us in advance about valuable shipments such as cash and jewellery.

        Do you have regular shipments?

        Let's set up a regular courier agreement for pharmacies, offices, shops and e-commerce. Tell us what you need and we will put together a plan.
