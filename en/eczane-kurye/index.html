Pharmacy Courier Istanbul

What is a pharmacy courier?

A pharmacy courier brings the medicine and health products prepared by the pharmacy to the patient's door. It removes the trouble of going to the pharmacy for patients, the elderly, people with disabilities, families with small children and anyone who cannot go out at night.

Kuryetex picks the order up from the pharmacy and hands it to the recipient in closed, well-packed form. Pharmacies can work with us for regular deliveries, or you can ask for your own pharmacy's package to be brought to your address.

What we pay attention to

  • The pharmacy prepares the package closed and sealed; the courier never opens it.
  • The package is carried so that it will not be crushed or get wet.
  • Delivery is made only at the stated address, to the recipient or the person they name.
  • For pharmacies with regular deliveries, agreed pricing is possible.
  • Medicine arrives within 30–45 minutes, depending on weather.

How an order works

  1. Tell us which medicine or product you need and your address.
  2. The courier goes to the pharmacy and collects it for you.
  3. It is handed to you at your door.
